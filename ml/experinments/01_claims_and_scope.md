# Research Claims and Scope

## Claim 1: System-Level Lifecycle Feasibility
An end-to-end ML lifecycle enables reliable transition from research to production for crop yield forecasting.

**Evidence Required**
- Deployment architecture
- API latency and reliability
- Versioning and retraining strategy
- Failure mode analysis

**Non-Claims**
- No guarantee of global scalability
- No claim of optimal infrastructure design

---

## Claim 2: Predictive Performance and Robustness
The proposed system achieves competitive predictive performance and maintains stability under environmental perturbations.

**Evidence Required**
- Baseline comparison (Mean, Linear Regression, Random Forest)
- Performance metrics (RMSE, MAE, R²)
- Stability under scenario perturbations

---

## Claim 3: Explainability Validity
Feature-level explanations are stable, interpretable, and practically useful.

**Evidence Required**
- SHAP rank stability
- Feature ablation impact
- Domain plausibility analysis

**Non-Claims**
- No claim of causal attribution
- No claim that explanations improve predictive accuracy

---

## Claim 4: Scenario-Based Decision Value
Scenario-based analysis reveals sensitivities not observable from point predictions.

**Evidence Required**
- Sensitivity curves
- Stress-testing results
- Monotonicity checks
